GMTK 2026 Game Jam Submission

Overview

Every year, Game Maker's Toolkit hosts one of the largest game jams, with this year seeing over 30,000 submissions. Despite having no prior game jam experience, a friend and I decided to participate. The work was split with him doing all the graphics, and I doing all the coding. In just 96 hours we were able to design, create, and publish our game. All the 3D models were custom made, and no AI code gen was used.
